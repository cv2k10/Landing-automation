# REVIEW — redesign-callout (38 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

## Creative calls

- **A call-out, not a demo:** the script talks straight to the owner ("Hey, business owner!
  Yes, you!"), names three pains, raises the stakes ("you're losing business"), makes the offer
  and ends on one action ("Reply 'redesign' now!"). On-screen text matches the voiceover, so the
  ad also works muted.
- **Big centred type over drawn illustrations.** No photos or third-party images. Ground colour
  carries the mood: yellow = call/offer, white = questions/proof, ink = problems/benefits,
  red = loss.
- **Transitions:** each scene floods its ground in a different way (right wipe, bottom wipe,
  centre iris, left wipe). The rocket takes off and pulls the final yellow up behind it.
- **Picture lands on the word:** megaphone waves on "Hey", calendar lands on 2014 as the
  question ends, gift lid pops on "offer", $0 tag on "free", the X strikes "picture", and the
  CTA button is clicked on "redesign".

## Measured vs looked at

- `hyperframes check`: passes with 0 errors and 0 warnings.
- All 19 beat frames looked at: every line is readable, nothing collides.
- Audio: voiceover levelled to about -17 LUFS per line, music bed about 10 dB under it, final mix
  normalised to -15 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP. **Measured, not heard.**

## Known limits / next steps

- The music bed is from MusicGen (non-commercial weights, CC-BY-NC 4.0): replace it with a
  licensed track before paid use.
- No brand name or logo is on the end card because none was supplied.
- Kokoro is a free local voice; a premium voice (HeyGen / ElevenLabs) would sound more
  energetic.
